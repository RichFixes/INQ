Booking, Payment & Service Terms
Section 1
Booking & Deposit Policy

A non-refundable deposit of 30% of the total service fee is required to secure your booking. This deposit reserves your shoot date exclusively. The remaining balance is due upon delivery of the final product. By paying the deposit, the client acknowledges that their shoot date is confirmed and that UwemMedia has turned away other bookings on their behalf.

Section 2
Cancellation & Rescheduling

Cancellations must be submitted in writing to UwemFilms@gmail.com at least 5 days prior to the scheduled shoot date. Cancellations made with proper notice may be eligible for a one-time credit toward a future UwemMedia booking at UwemMedia's sole discretion — no cash refunds will be issued. Cancellations within 5 days of the shoot date forfeit the deposit in full. Rescheduling is subject to availability and must be requested no less than 5 days in advance.

Section 3
No-Show Policy

Failure to appear at the scheduled shoot location within 30 minutes of the agreed start time without prior written notice constitutes a no-show. No-shows result in immediate forfeiture of the full deposit and UwemMedia reserves the right to decline future bookings from the client.
